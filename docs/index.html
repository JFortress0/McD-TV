Link your TV

Open Jarvis web app

Adding or removing an addon or website saves to the TV right away. For the other boxes, tap Save to TV. Changes are sent to your TV over the internet, encrypted end to end. Jarvis must be open on the TV to receive them, or they apply the next time it opens (within 12 hours).

      Your other TVs

      Copy once gives another TV (a friend's, say) a one-time copy of your setup without your Real-Debrid, and the TVs don't stay linked. TVs that share settings stay the same: change a key, an addon or the playlist on one, and the others update within seconds. Watch history and Continue Watching stay separate on each TV.
