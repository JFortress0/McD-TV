Connecting to your TV…

Link your TV

On the TV, open McD TV, go to the profile circle (top right), then Phone & Computer Setup. Scan the code with your phone's camera.

To use this page on a computer: open it from the QR code on your phone, tap Copy link, and open that link on the computer. You can also paste the link here:

Adding or removing an addon or website saves to the TV right away. For the other boxes, tap Save to TV. Changes are sent to your TV over the internet, encrypted end to end. McD TV must be open on the TV to receive them, or they apply the next time it opens (within 12 hours).
